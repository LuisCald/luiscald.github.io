    <p>Hello, and thank you for visiting! I am a <b>Postdoctoral Researcher</b> in economics at the <b>University of Bonn</b>, specializing in macroeconomics. I have completed my PhD only recently, also at the University of Bonn (<b>Summer 2026</b>).</p>
    <p>My primary interests emphasize the role of <b>household heterogeneity</b> for monetary and fiscal policy and macroeconomic dynamics. For this, I draw from an extensive tool kit that spans <b>Bayesian econometrics, functional data analysis, and semi-structural time series</b> models to fully structural approaches.</p>
    <p>I hold a PhD and a Master’s degree in Economics from Bonn and Bachelor’s degrees in Economics and Finance from Florida International University in Miami, FL.</p>
    <p>During my PhD at the University of Bonn, I was supervised by Christian Bayer, Farzad Saidi, Moritz Kuhn, and Benjamin Born.</p>
